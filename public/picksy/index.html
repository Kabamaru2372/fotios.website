New in v1.6

Detox together with a friend

Picksy is the only app that tells you when your friend is overusing their phone — at the same time as you. No accounts, no names, just anonymous nudges.

1
Send a challenge link to a friend — one tap from the app.
2
Your friend opens it and the pair is registered anonymously.
3
When both of you have been on your phones ≥20 min in the last 2h, you both get a notification.
4
Put them down together. It's easier when someone else does it with you.

Zero personal data. Friends are identified by random anonymous UUIDs — no names, no emails, no accounts. You can remove a connection any time from Settings.
